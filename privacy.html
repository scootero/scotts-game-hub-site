Privacy Policy

Scott's Game Hub · Last updated October 26, 2026

Scott's Game Hub and its games do not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.

Game progress and settings (such as best scores) are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

If you're signed in to Game Center on your device, your best scores are also sent to Apple's Game Center so they can appear on worldwide leaderboards under your Game Center nickname. This is optional and run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; we don't receive or store anything. To stop it, sign out of Game Center in the Settings app, where you can also manage or delete your Game Center data.

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple's handling of your purchase is covered by Apple's own privacy policy.
